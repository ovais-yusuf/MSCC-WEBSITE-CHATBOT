--- Overview ---
The Community One Stop for Growth is the main vehicle for public economic development investment in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. It combines 12 of the Commonwealth's most popular community-focused economic development and housing grant programs into a single application portal and collaborative review process.

The One Stop streamlines the experience for applicants and better coordinates economic development programs and staff on engagement and grant making. It also reorients the Commonwealth from a passive reviewer of funding requests to an active partner in economic development strategy, priorities, and investment.

The One Stop makes targeted investments based on a Development Continuum. It is a single application portal and collaborative review process of grant programs.

--- The Development Continuum ---
The One Stop framework is modeled around a Development Continuum that represents the life cycle of a major community development endeavor — from initial community visioning to final construction. The Development Continuum shapes the Full Application and serves as a guide to help applicants understand where their project falls within the One Stop, both in terms of timing and readiness.

--- One Stop Programs ---
The programs within the One Stop provide critical funding that spurs housing and economic development by investing in public infrastructure, preparing sites and cleaning up brownfields, and supporting vibrant downtowns. Visit individual program websites for program guidelines and full application templates.

Executive Office of Economic Development (EOED) programs:
- MassWorks Infrastructure Program: Funds public infrastructure improvements that support economic development and job creation.
- Massachusetts Downtown Initiative: Supports planning and implementation of downtown revitalization strategies.
- Rural Development Fund: Provides funding for rural communities to support economic development projects.
- Massachusetts Vacant Storefront Program: Helps fill vacant storefronts in commercial districts to support local economies.

Executive Office of Housing and Livable Communities (EOHLC) programs:
- Housing Choice Grant Program: Supports communities that are working to create more housing production and variety.
- Community Planning Grant Program: Funds planning work that advances housing production, climate resilience, and economic development.
- HousingWorks Infrastructure Program: Provides infrastructure funding to support housing development.

Mass Development programs:
- Underutilized Properties Program: Supports redevelopment of underutilized or vacant properties.
- Site Readiness Program: Prepares sites for development by funding pre-development activities.
- Brownfields Redevelopment Fund: Funds cleanup and redevelopment of contaminated properties (brownfields).
- Real Estate Services Technical Assistance: Provides technical assistance for real estate and development projects.
- TDI Equity Investment Program: Invests in transformative development initiatives focused on equity and community impact.

--- Application Guidance ---
Applicant guidance and education is a critical part of the One Stop process. Three main resources are available:

1. Expression of Interest (EOI): An optional, short form applicants can complete to learn whether their project is a fit for the One Stop. This is done before the full application.

2. Virtual Office Hours: To supplement guidance received in the EOI process, One Stop staff and program managers host virtual office hour sessions to answer applicant questions.

3. One Stop Webinar Series: Both live and on-demand webinars are available to help applicants better understand the One Stop application process as well as details about each program.

--- How to Apply ---
To apply for funding through the Community One Stop for Growth, applicants must complete and submit a Full Application through the IGX system.

- Applications may be created and worked on starting January 24, 2025.
- Applications may only be submitted from May 5, 2025 through June 4, 2025 (11:59 p.m. deadline).
- Once your organization is registered in IGX, you may begin work on any available opportunities for the current round.
- Any designated user may access, edit, and/or save an application on behalf of their organization.
- Only users with the "Grant Administrator" role will be able to create and submit the application.
