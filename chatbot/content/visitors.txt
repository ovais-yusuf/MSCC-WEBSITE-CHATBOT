--- Overview ---
The Metro South Region is a great place to live and do business. The area offers a wide variety of dining, shopping, hotels, parks, cultural attractions, sports, and more.

--- Shop Local ---
My Local MA is a statewide campaign sponsored by the Massachusetts Office of Travel and Tourism (MOTT) to encourage residents to choose local when they shop, dine, and travel.
More info: https://metrosouthchamber.com/visitors-guide/shop-local/
