RECENT NEWS ARTICLES (2025–2026):

1. Metro South Chamber Celebrates 11th Annual Multicultural Business Forum
   In March 2026, the Metro South Chamber of Commerce, in partnership with SCU Credit Union, hosted the 11th Annual Multicultural Business Forum & Business After Hours in Brockton. The event brought together business owners, professionals, and community leaders for an evening focused on entrepreneurship, resource-sharing, and regional economic growth. It featured a Women & Minority Owned Business Panel, a Business Resource Expo, and recognition from the Small Business Administration.
   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/

2. Everett's Auto Parts Celebrates 75 Years in Business in 2026!
   Everett's Auto Parts is celebrating 75 years in business — a milestone built on hard work, strong customer relationships, and a commitment to service. Over the decades, the team has adapted to changes in the automotive industry while staying focused on helping customers get back on the road quickly and efficiently.
   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/

3. Call for Nominations: Small Business & Women's Leadership Awards
   The Metro South Chamber is accepting nominations for the 35th Annual Small Business & Entrepreneur of the Year Awards and the 28th Annual ATHENA Leadership Award. The Small Business awards go to businesses that have shown employment staying power, growth, innovation, and social responsibility. The ATHENA Award recognizes an individual who has achieved excellence in their field and actively supported women in leadership.
   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/

4. Welcome New Members!
   The Metro South Chamber regularly welcomes new member businesses to its network of local leaders, entrepreneurs, and changemakers dedicated to growing the Metro South region.
   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/

5. Metro South Chamber Welcomes Ian Abreu as Director of Business Development & Engagement
   The Metro South Chamber of Commerce welcomed Ian Abreu as its new Director of Business Development & Engagement. Ian leads initiatives to strengthen regional business networks, enhance member engagement, and support economic growth throughout Metro South. He brings more than a decade of chamber work experience to the role.
   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/

7. New Investments Take Center Stage in Brockton as Mayor Highlights Pro-Business Growth
   The Metro South Chamber's February 2026 Good Morning Metro South breakfast brought members together in Brockton for a morning centered on leadership, investment, and the city's momentum. The program featured keynote remarks from Moises Rodrigues, Brockton's newly elected and first mayor of color, who shared his vision for the city's future including priorities around investment and pro-business growth.

8. Human Resources Panel at January Luncheon at Stonehill College
   Rockland Federal Credit Union joined the Metro South Chamber to kick off 2026 with an HR panel at Stonehill College's Meehan School of Business. The program brought together business leaders and regional specialists for a conversation on workforce, legal, and policy developments affecting employers, including topics on Senate Ways & Means.

9. D-BAT Year-Round Batting Practice Opens in Stoughton!

   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/

10. Seasonal Celebration Meets Business Insight at the December Holiday Luncheon
    The Metro South Chamber hosted its December Holiday Luncheon, sponsored by Cambridge Savings Bank, at the Fuller Craft Museum. The event featured speed networking, interviews with key regional figures, and insights from Jack Ferren, VP and Senior Small Business Relationship Manager at Cambridge Savings Bank. The event was emceed by Chamber Board Chair Dan Evans of EMI.

8. SBA Launches First-Ever Loan Program for American Manufacturers: MARC Loans (2025)
   The U.S. Small Business Administration launched its first-ever loan program dedicated to supporting small manufacturers — the 7(a) Manufacturer's Access to Revolving Credit (MARC) Loan Program. It offers working capital for businesses engaged in manufacturing to create jobs, onshore production, and expand.
   Read more: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/blog/
