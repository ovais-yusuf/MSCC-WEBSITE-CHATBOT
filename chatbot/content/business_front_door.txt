--- Overview ---
The Business Front Door is a Massachusetts state resource that connects businesses with the Executive Office of Economic Development and Team Massachusetts partner organizations. Once you get started, you'll be paired with a dedicated Team MA Navigator who helps you explore resources the state has to offer.

--- What is the Business Front Door? ---
Enter the Business Front Door to work with the Executive Office of Economic Development and Team Massachusetts partner organizations. A dedicated Team MA Navigator will help you explore state resources to make your business journey in Massachusetts a successful one.

More info: https://metrosouthchamber.com/business-front-door/

--- Why Massachusetts? ---
Massachusetts is an ideal place to start, scale, and grow your business. The state is home to cutting-edge innovation, high quality education, beautiful landscapes, winning sports teams, cultural attractions, and more.

--- Business Front Door Office Hours ---
Team MA hosts office hours on Tuesdays at 1pm starting March 18th, where businesses can ask questions and get guidance. Register at: https://metrosouthchamber.com/business-front-door/

- Closing a Business

- Job Incentives
- Professional Licenses, Permits, and Certificates
- Research & Commercialization
- Selling to the State
- Siting and Permitting for a Facility
- Starting or Moving a Business
- Transportation or Utility Infrastructure
- Workforce Support and Compliance
- Workforce, Internships & Training
- Working Capital, Bonds & Loans
