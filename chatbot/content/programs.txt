Metro South Chamber of Commerce — Partner Programs
URL: https://metrosouthchamber.com/metro-south-chamber-programs/

--- Greater Brockton Young Professionals ---
Greater Brockton Young Professionals (GBYP) are the next generation leaders creating a bright future for the Greater Brockton community. This program is run through the Metro South Chamber of Commerce.

--- Leadership Metro South ---
South Shore Bank has expanded into the city of Brockton, and with this exciting growth a partnership has been formed with the Metro South Chamber of Commerce to bring a Leadership Metro South program to the region. The program follows the same guidelines as the original South Shore program, with the focus to serve the communities in the Metro South footprint. Applications for this program opened in May of 2023.

--- Metro South Regional Economic Development Organization (REDO) ---
The objective of the Regional Economic Development Organization (REDO) program is to provide a consistent and efficient response to businesses seeking assistance from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. This program is managed through the Metro South Chamber of Commerce.

--- Metro South Wellness Works ---
The Metro South Chamber of Commerce has joined a cohort of Massachusetts employers dedicated to providing wellness education and support to employees to encourage healthy, happy, and productive workers and work sites.

--- Metro South Creative Economy ---
In Massachusetts, the creative industries include the many interlocking industry sectors that provide creative services or create and promote intellectual property products. The Metro South Creative Economy program supports these industries in the region.

--- Giving 110% Innovation Campaign ---
The Metro South Chamber of Commerce raised over $350,000 for modifications and updates to the 60 School Street building in Brockton, including a new roof, new carpets, fresh paint, updated technology, and other needed improvements.
