MSCC EVENTS

The Metro South Chamber of Commerce hosts a wide range of events to support business networking, professional development, and community engagement.

Types of events include:
- Business After Hours networking events
- Professional development workshops and seminars
- Ribbon cuttings for new and relocated businesses
- Annual award luncheons
- Career and workforce development events
- Economic development luncheons

Registration:
- Users can register through the MSCC Events page: https://metrosouthchamber.com/events/
- Some events are free, while others require a ticket fee ($39–$49 for most luncheons)
