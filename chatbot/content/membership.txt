MSCC MEMBERSHIP

Businesses can join the Metro South Chamber of Commerce through the Membership section of the website.

Membership Benefits:
- Access to new business prospects and networking opportunities
- Marketing and promotional exposure
- Access to business resources and information
- Discounts on services such as health and dental plans
- Opportunities to engage with the local business community

Joining the Chamber:
- To join, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/join/
- To view full member benefits, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/benefits/
- To browse the member directory, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/directory/
- For health insurance options available to members, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/health-insurance/
- For member discounts and savings, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/savings/
- For sponsorship opportunities, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/sponsorship/
- To renew membership, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/paypal/
- To log in as a member, visit: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/member-login/

For more details, users can contact the Chamber directly.

ADVERTISING OPPORTUNITIES

Advertising page: https://metrosouthchamber.com/membership/advertising/

Advertising with the Metro South Chamber gives your business exposure to the regional business community and helps target your marketing to business professionals and potential customers in Metro South.

For all advertising inquiries, contact Emma Penardi, VP of Communications & Special Projects:
- Phone: (508) 586-0500 ext. 222
- Email: [estratton@metrosouthchamber.com](mailto:estratton@metrosouthchamber.com)

1. Action Report Newsletter
   - Monthly 12-page publication
   - Combined audience of 11,000+ readers across print, digital, and email
   - 4,280 print readers via The Enterprise newspaper
   - Nearly 2,000 electronic edition readers
   - Distributed via weekly E-Update to 5,000+ subscribers (40%+ open rate)
   - Also distributed at Chamber office and regional events
   - Covers Chamber news, events, new members, announcements, member profiles, and Metro South stories
   - Members can submit press releases for the Good News section — send to [estratton@metrosouthchamber.com](mailto:estratton@metrosouthchamber.com)
   - For ad rates, contact The Enterprise: (508) 638-5585. Discounted rates available for members.
   - More info: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/action-report/

2. Metro South Guide to the Region (Book for Business)
   - Full-color, 100-page annual publication
   - Features Chamber member listings, town profiles, demographic and economic data, legislative updates, and local resources
   - Distributed to thousands of readers and posted online
   - Ads appear in bold with a dedicated advertiser index
   - Ad design assistance available from the Chamber's designer
   - Contact Emma at [estratton@metrosouthchamber.com](mailto:estratton@metrosouthchamber.com) or (508) 586-0500 ext. 222
   - More info: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/guide/

3. E-Update (Weekly Email Newsletter)
   - Reaches 5,000+ subscribers weekly
   - Consistently 40%+ open rate
   - Features upcoming events, legislative updates, services, and local resources
   - Clickable links to event registrations and member content
   - Members can submit content to the Community/Member Events section for free
   - Advertising starts at $25 per week
   - Download the e-Update advertising agreement: https://metrosouthchamber.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/eupdateadagreement2026.pdf
   - More info: https://metrosouthchamber.com/news-media/e-update/

4. Website Advertising
   - Advertise on metrosouthchamber.com — the go-to hub for members, businesses, tourists, and potential relocators
   - Over 6,000 unique visitors per month generating nearly 10,000 pageviews
   - Banner ads connect your business with potential customers, partners, and decision-makers
   - Download the website advertising agreement: https://metrosouthchamber.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/webadagreement2026.pdf

5. Maps
   - The Chamber designs and produces high-quality street and regional maps
   - Publications include: Brockton City Maps, a Regional Map, the Brockton History Trail Map, and the Bridgewaters Map
   - Each map includes street listings, libraries, post offices, police stations, schools, hospitals, and historical/cultural info
   - Maps are distributed at the Chamber office, at events, and mailed upon request

6. Brockton Information Boards (BIBs)
   - Large information boards installed in 8 high-traffic locations across Brockton
   - Locations include: Westgate Mall, Campanelli Stadium, the Brockton Area Transit Centre, and the downtown commuter rail station
   - Each board features local attractions, landmarks, and a detailed city map
   - Sponsor panel spaces available for high-visibility business placement
   - 2 new boards recently added
   - View BIBs brochure: https://metrosouthchamber.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/BIBS-Brochure-2026-2027-1.pdf

VIRTUAL PLAQUE

Virtual Plaque page: https://metrosouthchamber.com/plaque/

Chamber members can display a Virtual Plaque (digital badge) on their own website to show their pride in being a Metro South Chamber member and to link back to the Chamber's website.

Two badge styles are available:

1. Horizontal banner:
   - Link: https://bit.ly/vplaque1
   - Image URL: https://www.metrosouthchamber.com/images/msccmemberhorizontalweb.jpg
   - Size: 183 x 80 pixels

2. Vertical banner:
   - Link: https://bit.ly/vplaque2
   - Image URL: https://www.metrosouthchamber.com/images/msccmemberverticalweb.jpg
   - Size: 71 x 90 pixels

To add the virtual plaque, members choose a logo style and paste the provided HTML code into their website. Full instructions are available at: https://metrosouthchamber.com/plaque/
