--- Who We Are ---
Greater Brockton Young Professionals (GBYP) are next generation leaders creating a bright future for the Greater Brockton community. The GBYP is currently operating under the MassHire Greater Brockton Workforce Board.

--- Mission ---
"To create collaborative and engaging experiences in an inclusive environment for young professionals to network, learn and grow within the Greater Brockton community."

--- Values ---
The following values guide the actions and philosophy of the Greater Brockton Young Professionals:
- Diversity
- Inclusivity
- Professionalism
- Strong Mentor/Mentee Relationships
- Engagement
- Connectivity
- Community
- Economic Vibrancy
- Collaboration
- Personal Advancement
- Keep it FUN!
