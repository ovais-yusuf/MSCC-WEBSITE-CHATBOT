ABOUT MSCC

The Metro South Chamber of Commerce is a private, non-profit business organization based in Brockton, Massachusetts.

Purpose:
- Support and promote the local business community
- Provide leadership in advocacy, education, and networking
- Help drive economic development in the region

The Chamber connects businesses, community leaders, and organizations to create growth opportunities and strengthen the regional economy.

CHAMBER HISTORY

The Metro South Chamber of Commerce has a long and proud history rooted in the economic growth of the Brockton region and surrounding communities.

Founding purpose (February 14, 1913):
"The purpose of this chamber of commerce shall be to promote the commerce, trade, industry and public interest of the city of Brockton and surrounding region; to promote and regulate a commercial exchange; to acquire and disseminate business information; to establish and maintain uniformity in commercial usages; and to promote just and equitable principles of trade."

Key milestones:

- Late 1800s: The expansion of the shoe industry contributed greatly to the region's growth. Of Brockton's 431 factories, 91 were shoe-industry related, 68 were clothing, 35 were metal manufacturing, and 20 produced drugs and medicines. Other industries included wagons, carriages, machinery, and prepared foods. The region also developed railroad connections, electric street lighting, local newspapers, theaters, schools, concerts, and libraries.

- January 8, 1897: Founding of the Brockton Board of Trade, the precursor to the Chamber of Commerce.

- February 14, 1913: Business leaders, recognizing changing times and the need for greater economic diversity, formally created the Brockton Chamber of Commerce.

- 1960s: The Chamber supported master planning and urban renewal, and sponsored a citizens' survey that produced many benefits including the Brockton Area Multi-Services Center (BAMSI).

- 1970s: The Chamber underwent reorganization, adding several new divisions with an expanded overall mission.

- 1980s and 1990s: The Chamber purchased and renovated the historic Edison Power Station on School Street in Brockton. This building is now the permanent home of the Chamber of Commerce and houses meeting space and the Business Assistance Center.

The tradition of industry and entrepreneurial spirit has continued into the present across banking, health care, manufacturing, public services, utilities, media, insurance, real estate, and many other enterprises.

COMMITTEES

Committees page: https://metrosouthchamber.com/about-the-chamber/committees/

The Metro South Chamber has several active committees and forums that members can get involved in:

1. Ambassador Team
   Ensures the Chamber is responsive to member needs through personal contact and networking. Ambassadors act as representatives of the Chamber at events and invite prospective members to join.
   - Meets monthly, generally on the first Wednesday of the month at the Chamber (unless otherwise stated)
   - To join: call (508) 586-0500 x221 or email [info@metrosouthchamber.com](mailto:info@metrosouthchamber.com)

2. A Taste of Metro South Planning Committee
   Organizes a community event to increase awareness and appreciation of living and working in the Metro South region. Area restaurants offer tastes of their house specialties, social service organizations set up exhibits, and cultural organizations provide displays and performances.

3. Good Morning Metro South Planning Committee
   Organizes the monthly breakfast program by developing ideas for topics and featured segments.

4. Government & Legislative Committee
   Reviews pending legislation and regulations for their impact on business, and develops positions to enhance economic growth. Sponsors programs that educate and encourage members to participate in the Chamber's government affairs initiatives.

5. Awards Committees
   Members participate on the Small Business of the Year Award or ATHENA Award Program Committees to help acknowledge and recognize leaders within the region.

To participate on any committee, contact the Chamber at (508) 586-0500.

CHAMBER FOUNDATION

Foundation page: https://metrosouthchamber.com/about-the-chamber/chamber-foundation/

The Metro South Foundation was established by the Metro South Chamber of Commerce to assist in the betterment of the community through charitable business education activities.

What the Foundation funds:
- Small business and entrepreneurial education and counseling
- Workforce development projects in the Greater Brockton region
- Support for businesses located in federally identified low- and moderate-income census tracts where the Chamber headquarters is located
- Training programs and nonprofits focused on vocational, technical, and entrepreneurial services

Donations:
- All donations to the Chamber Foundation are tax deductible.
- To donate, call (508) 586-0500 ext. 223 (Christopher Cooney, President & CEO)
