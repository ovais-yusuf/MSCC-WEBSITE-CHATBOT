Metro South Chamber of Commerce — Leadership Metro South
URL: https://metrosouthchamber.com/leadership-metro-south/

--- Overview ---
Leadership Metro South is a yearlong immersive community leadership program run in partnership between South Shore Bank and the Metro South Chamber of Commerce. It was established as an extension of Leadership South Shore (founded in 2016), brought to the Metro South region in 2023.

The program selects participants from a pool of applicants and leads them through a yearlong immersive learning curriculum, resulting in a group of individuals committed to transformative leadership in the community. More than 160 highly motivated graduates have completed the program.

"Community leadership is the courage, creativity and capacity to inspire participation, development and sustainability for strong communities." — Gustav Nossal

--- Why a Leadership Program? ---
Leaders and emerging leaders play a vital role in the workplace and in communities. It is important that they understand what is unique to their region and learn to navigate and embrace opportunities and challenges so they can help build, define, and promote the regions in which they work and call home. Informed leaders make informed decisions.

--- Program Format ---
The cohort convenes 8 times over a year, beginning in September. The program runs from October through May, with a September orientation and graduation. Participants attend one program day per month during fall and spring. Attendance at all program days is required. Applicants must also submit an Employer Commitment Form.

Program days are thought-provoking and examine regional challenges and opportunities through expert panel discussions, virtual and on-site visits, presentations, focus groups, interactive activities, and other innovative experiential activities.

--- Program Day Themes ---
- Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEIB): Healthy workplace cultures, supporting marginalized employees, DEI awareness strategies.
- Health & Human Services: Addiction crisis, homelessness, food shortages, mental health, workforce shortages in healthcare.
- Education System: Post-secondary options, STEM access, early education, changing face of K-12.
- Justice System: Inmate programming, K9 demonstrations, crime lab analysis, drug court, community policing.
- Economic Development: Regional economic development, real estate, manufacturing, effects of COVID on business growth, housing shortage.
- Arts & Culture: Impact of arts on economic development, hospitality and tourism, creative arts therapies.

--- Alumni ---
Alumni serve on the Program Planning Committees to help shape future learning experiences and keep the program relevant. Graduates have formed new partnerships and collaborations and have become actively involved in their communities through volunteerism, board positions, and civic and political service.

--- How to Apply ---
Each year a diverse group of emerging leaders is selected. There is a tuition fee of $500, which covers all program-related activities, transportation, and materials. There are limited seats available and participation is generally limited to one person per organization.

Applications for the 2025–2026 cohort opened April 2, 2025 and closed May 2, 2025. Each applicant is invited to an in-person interview with the program's Steering Committee in May. Selections are announced mid-June.
